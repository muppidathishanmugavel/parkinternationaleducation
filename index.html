Verify Before You Pay: 4 Mandatory Checks

Protect your career and hard-earned savings by verifying these 4 critical items before making any financial commitment:

1. Exact Degree Title & Course Duration

Confirm the exact medical or engineering degree awarded by the university and ensure total program duration matches regulatory requirements.

2. NMC FMGL Regulations Compliance

For MBBS abroad, verify that the program adheres to the 54 months of theoretical/practical study plus 12 months internship in the same institution in English medium.

3. Itemized Fee Schedule in Writing

Never accept verbal assurances. Require a signed breakdown of tuition fees per year, hostel, food, medical insurance, and university charges.

4. Written Refund & Cancellation Policy

Understand official refund terms in the event of unforeseen visa or personal complications before remitting registration or processing fees.

Frequently Asked Questions அடிக்கடி கேட்கப்படும் கேள்விகள்

Clear, regulatory-compliant answers to help students and parents make well-informed decisions. மருத்துவம் மற்றும் உயர் கல்வி சேர்க்கை குறித்த நம்பகமான பதில்கள்.

Can an Indian student study MBBS abroad after +2? What are the basic requirements?
Yes. Indian students who have completed +2 (Physics, Chemistry, and Biology) with the prescribed minimum percentage and qualified in the NEET-UG examination are eligible to pursue MBBS abroad. The chosen foreign university must adhere to National Medical Commission (NMC) guidelines regarding total program duration, English-medium curriculum, clinical rotations, and license eligibility in the host country.
Will my international MBBS degree be recognized to practice medicine in India?
Foreign medical graduates are eligible to practice in India upon qualifying in the National Exit Test (NExT) / Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) and completing the required internship as regulated by the National Medical Commission. We verify that each university recommended by PARK Education strictly complies with current NMC Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate (FMGL) regulations.
Which country offers the most affordable MBBS education without compromising quality?
Destinations such as Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, and Russia offer government-subsidized medical tuition fees that are significantly lower than private medical colleges in India. However, total expenses depend on the specific institution, hostel quality, and living standards. We provide an exact, written fee comparison for all suitable options during your consultation.
Does PARK Education assist students with bank education loans?
Yes. We provide complete documentation support, including official admission letters, university-attested fee structures, and course estimates required by nationalized and private Indian banks to evaluate and process education loan applications.
Are hostel facilities and Indian food available for students studying abroad?
Yes. The partner universities we recommend offer secure on-campus or affiliated student hostels with separate wings for boys and girls, 24/7 security, heating, Wi-Fi, and dedicated Indian mess facilities serving both South Indian and North Indian meals.
What other professional courses does PARK Education guide besides MBBS?
Beyond medicine, we provide direct admissions counselling for B.E./B.Tech Engineering, B.Sc & GNM Nursing, Law (LL.B / Integrated B.A. LL.B), Paramedical Sciences, Allied Health Sciences, Pharmacy (D.Pharm / B.Pharm / Pharm.D), Aviation & Pilot Training, Arts & Science collegiate degrees, and Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) in recognized institutions across India.
